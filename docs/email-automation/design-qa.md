- [P1 fixed] Tenant and landlord receipts now use separate approved banners (`02` and `03`) while retaining the same stored invoice facts and payment amount.
- [P1 fixed] Partial, under, over, ambiguous, and unmatched SePay states route to their red/amber approved banners and never claim full payment.
- [P2 fixed] Invoice lines use right-aligned amounts and a highlighted remaining balance row for readability in Gmail.

## Required fidelity surfaces

- Fonts: Arial/Helvetica fallback is used consistently in Gmail-safe inline styles; approved banner artwork carries the display typography.
- Spacing/layout: 600px email shell with responsive 480px padding; all 17 rendered emails passed the 640px overflow check.
- Colors/tokens: banner and body semantic colors follow the approved green, red, amber, blue, and purple workflow states.
- Image quality: each approved banner is sent as its matching inline CID PNG; the MIME test verifies the bytes against the packaged asset for all 17 banners.
- Copy/content: Vietnamese copy follows the approved demos, including the corrected “Đến kỳ thanh toán” wording and direct invoice details on receipts.
